\section{Appendix Guide}
Briefly describe what each appendix section contains and which main-text claims it supports.

\section{Reproducibility Details}
Environment, hardware, seeds, compute budget, software versions, checkpoints, and exact run commands.

\section{Dataset and Protocol Details}
Dataset source, licenses, preprocessing, splits, leakage checks, metric definitions, and candidate construction.

\section{Baseline Details}
Official implementations, checkpoints, hyperparameters, reproduction deviations, and fairness notes.

\section{Additional Ablations and Robustness}
Large result grids, extra seeds, stress tests, sensitivity analyses, and negative results.

\section{Derivations or Proofs}
Theorems, assumptions, proof sketches, and detailed derivations.

\section{Qualitative Examples and Failure Cases}
Selection criteria, success cases, failure cases, and boundary conditions.

\section{Additional Figures and Tables}
Supplementary visuals with source-data pointers.
